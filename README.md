# AI Health Care – Doctor AI 🩺

**AI Health Care – Doctor AI** is a comprehensive digital healthcare platform that connects patients with doctors through a secure chat-based consultation system. The platform consists of a **Flutter mobile application for users** and a **web-based interface for doctors and administrators**.

The system allows users to consult doctors online, share medical images and prescriptions, receive personalized health guidance, monitor their health, and manage various healthcare activities from a single platform.

## 🌟 Main Features

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor–Patient Consultation

* Users can browse and connect with doctors.
* Patients can communicate directly with doctors through chat.
* Users can send **medical images, reports, and prescriptions** to doctors for analysis.
* Doctors can review the patient's information and medical reports before providing guidance.
* Doctors can respond to patients and provide healthcare recommendations.

### 💳 Online Consultation & Payment

* Consultation access is unlocked after successful **Razorpay payment**.
* Once payment is completed, the user receives access to the doctor chat.
* The consultation remains active for **24 hours**.
* After 24 hours, the consultation automatically closes and the chat is locked.

### 📋 Prescription & Medical Reports

* Users can upload and share prescriptions with doctors.
* Patients can send medical reports and images.
* Doctors can analyze the submitted reports and provide appropriate guidance.
* Medical information can be reviewed as part of the consultation.

### 📊 Health Monitoring

* Health monitoring features help users keep track of important health-related activities.
* Users can receive health-related reminders, alerts, and recommendations.
* The platform is designed to provide personalized health guidance based on user information.

### 🥗 Diet Plan

* Personalized diet plans can be created for users.
* Diet recommendations can be provided based on the user's health requirements.
* Users can access their diet-related guidance through the application.

### 👩 Women's Health

The platform includes dedicated women's health features such as:

* Period tracking
* Next-period prediction
* Menstrual-cycle monitoring
* Women's health tips
* Personalized health-related information

### 💧 Dehydration Alerts

* Users can receive hydration reminders and dehydration alerts.
* The feature encourages users to maintain healthy water-intake habits.

### ⭐ Doctor Ratings & Reviews

* Users can rate doctors based on their consultation experience.
* Patients can provide feedback about doctor performance and response time.
* Ratings and reviews help maintain transparency in the doctor–patient experience.

### 🛡️ Admin Management

* Administrators can manage users and doctors.
* Admins can review and verify doctor information/reviews.
* The administration system helps maintain the quality and reliability of the platform.

## 📱 User Application

The patient-facing application is developed using **Flutter**, allowing users to:

* Register and manage their profile
* Connect with doctors
* Make online payments
* Start 24-hour consultations
* Chat with doctors
* Upload medical images and reports
* Share prescriptions
* Receive diet plans
* Track periods
* Receive health tips and hydration alerts
* Rate and review doctors

## 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Platform

Doctors can:

* Manage their profile
* View patient consultation requests
* Chat with patients
* Receive medical images and reports
* Review prescriptions
* Analyze patient-submitted reports
* Provide health guidance
* Manage active consultations

## 👨‍💼 Admin Platform

The administrator can:

* Manage doctors and users
* Verify doctor information
* Monitor platform activity
* Review doctor feedback
* Manage ratings and reviews
* Help maintain the overall quality of the healthcare platform

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Mobile Application:** Flutter / Dart
* **Doctor & Admin Interface:** Web Application
* **Backend:** Python
* **Database:** Project-dependent database services
* **Payment Gateway:** Razorpay
* **AI/Health Assistance:** Google Gemini API
* **Version Control:** Git & GitHub

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of AI Health Care – Doctor AI is to provide a convenient digital platform for connecting patients with doctors while combining online consultation, medical document sharing, health monitoring, women's health tracking, diet planning, hydration reminders, and AI-assisted healthcare features.

The project demonstrates how modern mobile applications, online payments, real-time communication, and artificial intelligence can be integrated into a unified healthcare platform.

> **Medical Disclaimer:** This application is intended to support communication, health tracking, and access to general healthcare information. It is not intended to replace professional medical diagnosis or emergency medical care. Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions.
